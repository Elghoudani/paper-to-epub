Paper to EPUB

Two-column research papers, converted into EPUBs that reflow on a 6-inch e-reader. Body text stays text; every figure, table, plot and equation is rendered from the PDF as an image, so the maths arrives intact.

This runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
